- Remaining risks or follow-ups:
  1. **Ad attribution is only as good as the Page subscription.** Meta delivers
     `message.referral` only when the Page is subscribed to
     `messaging_referrals` *and* `messages`; a Page connected before that
     change must be disconnected/reconnected once, otherwise Meta omits the
     referral silently and the new column simply stays blank. Worth confirming
     in the admin console.
  2. **UTM parameters do not apply to Messenger ads.** Meta appends `utm_*` to
     website destinations only. The working mechanism for Messenger is the
     `ref` parameter on the m.me link (`https://m.me/<PAGE_ID>?ref=CODE`),
     resolved against `projects.slug` / `projects.aliases`, with `ad_title` as
     the fallback. Whoever configures the ads needs this.
  3. **Not verified against production data.** The new column's contents were
     not checked against live rows; confirm on the next preview send.
  4. **Accepted, unchanged:** preview enrichment remains sequential and may
     make up to `MAX_CANDIDATES_PER_DIGEST` LLM calls in one request; the
     workbook filename/banner still carries the send date rather than the
     window date.
